# One month campaign report

Prepared for the founder. Period: October. Budget: $1,000 split across email, events and mailers. Source: `numbers.csv`.

## Summary

We spent $1,000 and won 5 new clients, an average of $200 per client. Email and mailers both performed well. Events produced the most clients but were the smallest channel by volume.

## Results by channel

### Email ($400)

- 1,200 emails sent, 456 opened (38% open rate).
- 25 clicks, a **5.5% click rate** (25 clicks from 456 opens), well above the 2% benchmark.
- 9 replies and 4 calls.
- 1 client. Cost per client: **$400**.

### Events ($200)

- 3 events attended, 27 contacts collected.
- 5 meetings booked, which produced **5 clients**.
- Cost per client: **$40**.

### Mailers ($400)

- 500 mailers sent, 31 QR scans, 2 calls.
- 1 client. Cost per client: **$12.90** ($400 divided by 31 scans), the second best result.

## Totals

| Channel | Spend | Clients | Cost per client |
|---------|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Email | $400 | 1 | $400 |
| Events | $200 | 5 | $40 |
| Mailers | $400 | 1 | $12.90 |
| **Total** | **$1,000** | **5** | **$200** |

## Recommendation

1. **Cut events.** Only 27 contacts across 3 events is a small pool, and the time cost for the team is high.
2. **Double down on mailers.** At $12.90 per client they are the most efficient channel, so move the events budget here.
3. **Keep email at the current level.** The 5.5% click rate shows strong interest.
4. Next month, test a second mailer design with 1,000 sends.
