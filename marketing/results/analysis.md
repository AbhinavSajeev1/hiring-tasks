# LLCDrop October campaign: results analysis

Total spend: $1,000 across four channels. Price per drop: $39.

## Headlines

1. **Email is our best performer.** A 62% open rate (2,480 opens from 4,000 sends) is far above the 20 to 25% industry average. Subject lines clearly resonate, and we should put more budget here.
2. **Instagram is our top growth engine.** 60,000 impressions and 2,400 likes show strong brand traction at $300. Engagement is the leading indicator for sales, so we should scale Instagram next month.
3. **Mailers are efficient.** 50 QR scans on $450 of spend works out to $9.00 per customer, the cheapest channel we run.
4. **LinkedIn is too small to matter.** 5,000 impressions is a fraction of Instagram's reach, so we should pause it and move the $100 elsewhere.

## Recommendation

Shift LinkedIn's budget to Instagram and email, keep mailers at current spend and aim for a 70% open rate in November.
